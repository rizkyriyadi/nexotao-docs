# Models & Pricing

Phase 1 has **32 active Amazon Bedrock models** from 12 model providers. All use
one Nexotao key and one Rupiah balance.

## Pricing policy

Nexotao retail rates are a **manual snapshot**, calculated as **100% of AWS
Bedrock US Standard on-demand pricing** and converted at **Rp 18,000/USD**. The
rates match that official US Standard reference; they do not automatically
follow a single worldwide or "global" price.

The production catalog is the source of truth. Read it before making cost
assumptions:

```bash
curl -s https://api.nexotao.com/models | jq '.models[] | {
  model, display_name, provider, supports_tools, supports_vision,
  context_window, input_per_million_micro, output_per_million_micro
}'
```
